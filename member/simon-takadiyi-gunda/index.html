Biography

Simon Takadiyi GUNDA is a Registered Diagnostic Radiographer and Sonographer, and an Educator in the Radiography discipline with the National University of Science and Technology (NUST) of Zimbabwe. He received his bachelors (Hons) degree in Radiography in 2006 and a master’s degree in Radiography in 2013. He also holds a second master’s degree in medical Ultrasonography in 2021 (NUST-Zimbabwe). Currently he is a PhD candidate with The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (SAR, China) and his research interests are in Ultrasonography assessment of haemodynamic and morphological features in post stroke patients, utilizing recent advances in Ultrasound imaging techniques.
