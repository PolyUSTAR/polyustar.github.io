Biography

Jingguo Qu received his bachelor’s (BEng) and master’s degrees (MSc) in civil engineering and computer science in 2018 and 2023, respectively. He is currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Health Technology and Informatics, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research interests include medical image processing, deep learning, and computer-aided diagnosis. He likes hiking and is a traveling enthusiast (Blog posts: 2024, 2025), and he also is an amateur photographer (Unsplash portfolio).
