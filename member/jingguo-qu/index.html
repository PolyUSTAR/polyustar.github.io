Biography

Jingguo Qu received his B.Eng. degree from Eastern Liaoning University in 2018 and his M.Eng. degree from Southwest Petroleum University in 2023. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in the Department of Health Technology and Informatics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research interests include medical image processing, deep learning, and computer-aided diagnosis. He likes hiking and is a traveling enthusiast (Blog posts: 2024, 2025), and he also is an amateur photographer (Unsplash portfolio).
